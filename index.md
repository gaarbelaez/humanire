# HUMANIRE

## Human-centred design and ergonomics for industrial reconfigurable systems

**HUMANIRE** is a 48-month research project focused on integrating **Extended Reality (XR)** into the design and ergonomic validation of **reconfigurable manufacturing systems**, with particular attention to the needs of SMEs.

The project brings together academic and industrial partners to develop and validate practical methods connecting **CAD, digital twins, XR and ergonomic assessment**.

**ANR AAPG 2026 · PRCE · 48 months**

## Why HUMANIRE?

Industrial systems increasingly need to evolve quickly while remaining safe, efficient and adapted to the people who operate them. HUMANIRE investigates how XR can become an integral part of the design process rather than an isolated technology used only at the end of development.

The project addresses three central challenges:

- integrating XR into the design of reconfigurable production systems;
- connecting industrial data such as CAD and digital twins with XR environments;
- embedding ergonomic validation and human-centred design into iterative industrial design processes.

## What will the project deliver?

HUMANIRE aims to produce:

- a methodology for integrating XR into the design of reconfigurable manufacturing systems;
- interoperability mechanisms between industrial digital tools and XR environments;
- validated XR-based ergonomic assessment approaches;
- an integrated, user-centred design framework;
- two industrial proofs of concept demonstrating the approach in real contexts;
- open scientific publications, datasets and software contributions where possible.

## A research project with industrial application

The project is conducted through close collaboration between research laboratories and industrial partners. Its approach combines scientific development, experimentation and industrial validation.
