# Work packages

HUMANIRE is organised into six work packages covering project management, needs analysis, scientific development, industrial validation and dissemination.

## WP0 — Governance and project management

**T0 → T0+48**

Coordinate the consortium, monitor scientific progress and ensure effective collaboration between academic and industrial partners.

## WP1 — Analysis of SME needs for XR adoption

**T0 → T0+24**

Identify the needs, barriers and requirements associated with the adoption of XR for ergonomic validation and production-system design in SMEs.

## WP2a — XR integration in the design of reconfigurable production systems

**T0+6 → T0+30**

Develop methods for integrating XR into the design and reconfiguration of industrial systems, including the transfer of relevant CAD and digital-twin information.

## WP2b — XR-based ergonomic validation

**T0+6 → T0+30**

Develop and validate an XR-based approach for assessing and improving the ergonomics of reconfigurable workstations.

## WP3 — Towards a closed, user-centred design process

**T0+24 → T0+48**

Bring together the developments from WP2 into an integrated framework with a unified information-interoperability layer and user-dependent views.

## WP4 — Industrial proofs of concept

**T0+36 → T0+48**

Demonstrate the integrated framework in two contrasted industrial contexts and evaluate its applicability in real conditions.

## WP5 — Dissemination and training

**T0 → T0+48**

Share scientific results and develop accessible resources, workshops and dissemination activities aimed particularly at SMEs and the broader XR ecosystem.
