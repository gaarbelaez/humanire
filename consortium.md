# Consortium

HUMANIRE brings together research laboratories and industrial partners with complementary expertise in XR, ergonomics, human-centred design and reconfigurable manufacturing.

## Academic partners

### ICube · Université de Strasbourg

Expertise in ergonomics, human-centred design, XR tools and evaluation frameworks.

### LISPEN · Arts et Métiers

Expertise in immersive technologies, human factors, XR interaction and CAD-to-XR model adaptation.

### LCFC · Arts et Métiers

Expertise in reconfigurable manufacturing systems, industrial engineering and co-design of products and production systems.

## Industrial partners

### Baumert

Industrial partner providing a manufacturing and assembly context in which workstation reconfiguration and ergonomic validation can be studied.

### CMS Automatisme

Industrial partner specialised in the design and integration of industrial systems, including automated production lines and robotic cells.

## Strategic dissemination partners

HUMANIRE also benefits from the involvement of strategic partners contributing to outreach, industrial dissemination and the wider XR ecosystem.

- **EKS-Intec**
- **France Immersive Learning**
