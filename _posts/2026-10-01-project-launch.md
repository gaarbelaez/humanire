HUMANIRE is entering its 48-month research programme on human-centred design and ergonomics for industrial reconfigurable systems.

The project will investigate how Extended Reality can be integrated into industrial design processes and ergonomic validation, with a particular focus on SME contexts.

The consortium brings together research laboratories and industrial partners around XR, ergonomics, human-centred design and reconfigurable manufacturing.
